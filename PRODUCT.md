# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners, business operators, and property teams looking for general pest or termite control for residential and commercial properties.

## Product Purpose

DMSA Pest Control Services helps customers understand its pest-control services, request a quotation, share a property location and pest concern, and contact the team. A useful enquiry gives DMSA enough context to follow up and confirm scope, timing, and price.

## Positioning

The site presents an inspection-led quotation process. A free ocular inspection is included in the quotation process; the service scope and price are confirmed after the property needs are reviewed. No exclusive market differentiator has been established.

## Operating Context

Customers can request a quotation online, contact DMSA by phone or Viber, or send a website message. An on-site inspection may be arranged during quotation follow-up. The site lists service coverage across the Bicol Region, Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, Quezon, and Metro Manila.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services include general pest control, termite control, residential pest control, and commercial pest control.
- Quote and contact forms send email; this project does not store their submissions.
- Map pins are generalized and must not reveal exact customer addresses.
- Customer feedback is published only after permission and approval. Public reviews use general location labels and omit customer names.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the DMSA Pest Control Services name and supplied logo.
- Preserve the existing wine-red, charcoal, white, and neutral-gray palette, as requested by the user.
- Keep public copy clear, professional, and grounded in confirmed service details.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing service descriptions, coverage details, quote flow, contact details, and supplied DMSA photography are in the project.
- Gallery records and images are marked as sample content; do not attach unapproved locations, outcomes, or customer statements to them.
- The testimonials data is empty. No permission-cleared customer quotes or ratings were provided for this revision.

## Product Principles

- Make it easy to describe a property concern and request a quotation.
- Review the property before confirming service scope and price.
- Protect customer privacy in maps and public feedback.
- Use approved customer feedback and verified service details only.
