## 3. Asking for reviews

Ask every customer within a day of the job. Use the review link from your Business Profile dashboard ("Ask for reviews"). Reply to every review, good or bad, within a few days.

**English:**

> Hi [Name], thank you for choosing DMSA Pest Control Services! If you were happy with the [termite treatment / pest control] at your home in [town], we'd really appreciate a short Google review. It helps families in [town] find us: [review link]

**Taglish:**

> Hi [Name], salamat po sa pagtitiwala sa DMSA Pest Control Services! Kung satisfied po kayo sa [anay treatment / pest control] sa [town], pwede po ba kayong mag-iwan ng maikling Google review? Malaking tulong po ito: [review link]

Reviews that mention the service and town naturally ("anay treatment in Calamba") help most. Never offer discounts or gifts for reviews, and never write reviews yourself; Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

Once there are real reviews, send me a few to publish (with the customer's permission). The `/testimonials` page then goes into Google.
